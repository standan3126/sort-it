How to play Pour

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour into it.

Pouring
The top colour pours onto the same colour, or into an empty tube, as much as fits.
Done
Every tube holds one colour (or nothing).
Undo
5 each level. Use them well.
Stuck?
With no moves and no undos left, start the level again. Restart is always free.
Your score
When you finish, you see your moves next to the fewest moves possible. Every pour counts, even one you undo. Can you match it?
The clock
Counts only while the tubes are in front of you.

How to play Untangle

Drag a peg at the bottom to an empty hole. The peg floats above your finger; when a hole lights up yellow, let go.

Moving a rope
The rope you move always passes over the ropes it crosses. The order you move them in matters.
Done
Every rope hangs free, with nothing wound around it.
Undo
3 each level. An undo takes the move back off your count.
Cut
You earn a Cut at level 50, and one more every 50 levels; unused ones save up. A Cut takes a rope out and counts as a move, and that level can't be Perfect. Restart gives the Cut back.
Your score
When you finish, you see your moves next to the fewest moves possible. Can you match it?
