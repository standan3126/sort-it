How to play Pour

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour into it.

Pouring
The top colour pours onto the same colour, or into an empty tube, as much as fits.
Done
Every tube holds one colour (or nothing).
Undo
5 each level. Use them well.
Stuck?
With no moves and no undos left, start the level again. Restart is always free.
Your score
When you finish, you see your moves next to the fewest moves possible. Every pour counts, even one you undo. Can you match it?
The clock
Counts only while the tubes are in front of you.
